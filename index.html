About Me

I'm a student developer at LSU New Orleans driven by a belief that technology should serve people — especially those who need it most.

My current project, The Path, is a full-stack recovery and support web app built with Flask, SQLite, and Python. It features sobriety tracking, daily mood check-ins, and a faith-inspired design — built to make a real difference.

I'm actively growing my skills through Codecademy, hands-on projects, and mentorship. I'm preparing for PowerUp 2027 in Denver — ready to connect with industry professionals and land an internship that lets me keep building things that matter.

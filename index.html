About Me

I'm a Computer Science student at LSU New Orleans with a focus on software development and enterprise systems. I build full-stack web applications, work with SQL and Python, and am actively deepening my knowledge of Java and core CS fundamentals.

Before tech, I spent years in job site supervision and landscaping — managing projects, leading crews, and solving problems under pressure. That background gave me something most developers are still building: a real work ethic and the ability to deliver. I've also worked in digital marketing and built custom workstations, which rounds out my perspective on both the business and technical sides of the industry.

My current project, The Path, is a full-stack recovery and support app built with Flask, SQLite, and Python — designed with purpose and built from scratch. Outside of that, I'm expanding into enterprise environments, exploring how SQL and modern tooling support large-scale systems.

Long term, I'm moving toward product or project management — but right now I'm heads-down building, learning, and contributing. If you're working on something meaningful, I'm always open to a conversation.
